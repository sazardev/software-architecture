# 04 · If your team is organized this way, your system will be too

Reference article: `/en/docs/ley-de-conway-team-topologies`

---

Draw your org chart. You already know what your architecture looks like.

Melvin Conway wrote it in 1968: organizations design systems that copy their communication structure.

Three teams will produce three big pieces, even if nobody designed it that way. And if every change goes through five teams, your architecture will have five coordination points.

The useful move is the reverse: the inverse Conway maneuver. Design the value flow and the teams that sustain it first, and let the architecture follow.

Team Topologies gives you a vocabulary:

- Four team types: stream-aligned, platform, enabling and complicated-subsystem.
- Three interaction modes: collaboration, X-as-a-Service and facilitation.

And don't forget cognitive load: a team can only own so much domain.

Does your architecture reflect your teams, or do your teams reflect your architecture?
